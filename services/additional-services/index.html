Vacation Leave

Typical Use

  • Personal travel, family activities, or scheduled rest days.
  • Planned absences requested in advance by municipal workers.

Common Requirements

  • Approved leave form signed by the immediate supervisor.
  • Updated leave balance from the records or HR office.
  • Endorsement for proper work turnover before the leave dates.

Sick Leave

Typical Use

  • Absence due to illness, recovery, or medical consultation.
  • May also be used when a worker is medically unfit for duty.

Common Requirements

  • Accomplished leave application or post-leave submission if urgent.
  • Medical certificate or supporting health document when required.
  • Supervisor notification within office policy timelines.

Maternity / Paternity Leave

Typical Use

  • Leave for childbirth, postnatal recovery, or support to a spouse after delivery.
  • Used by eligible municipal employees under applicable leave rules.

Common Requirements

  • Leave form with expected leave dates.
  • Medical certificate, ultrasound result, or birth-related supporting document.
  • Supervisor and records office confirmation of eligibility.

Special Privilege Leave

Typical Use

  • Important personal transactions, family events, or urgent appointments.
  • Short approved leave used by workers within available entitlement.

Common Requirements

  • Accomplished leave request with brief purpose of absence.
  • Supervisor approval before the requested date whenever possible.
  • Verification of remaining leave credits, if applicable.

Leave Without Pay

Typical Use

  • Extended absence when available leave credits have been used.
  • May be requested for personal, medical, or family-related reasons.

Common Requirements

  • Formal written request stating the reason and exact leave period.
  • Approval from the department head or authorized office.
  • Work endorsement and acknowledgment of pay impact during absence.
