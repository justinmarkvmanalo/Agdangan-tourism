About the Office

Voter registration and election-related frontline services for residents.

The Commission on Elections (COMELEC) is the premier guardian of the ballot. Its principal role is to enforce and administer all laws and regulations relative to the conduct of elections, plebiscites, initiatives, referenda and recall.

Application for Registration

Requirements

  • Employee's ID with the signature of employer or authorized representative; Postal ID;
  • PWD Discount ID;
  • Student's ID or library card signed by the school authority;
  • Senior Citizen's ID;
  • Driver's License;
  • NBI Clearance;
  • Passport;
  • SSS/GSIS ID;
  • Integrated Bar of the Philippines (IBP) ID;
  • License issued by the Professional Regulatory Board (PRC);
  • Certificate of Confirmation issued by the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) in case of members of ICCs of IPs;
  • Any other valid ID

Photocopy of the Valid ID Presented

  • Photocopy of the valid ID presented

Application for Transfer of Registration Record

Requirements

  • Any proof of registration:
  • Voter ID
  • Certificate from the Election Officer
  • Certificate from the National Central File
  • Any valid ID previously enumerated that bear applicant's photograph and signature
  • Proof of residency

Steps

  • Register in visitor's logbook
  • Submission of request letter indicating the data needed and the purpose of the request or data request form for email transactions

Application for Reactivation of Registration Record

Requirements

  • Any valid ID previously enumerated that bear applicant's photograph and signature

Application for Change of Name

Requirements

  • Original/certified true copy of any of the following:
  • Certification by the solemnizing officer
  • Marriage contract or court order with certificate of finality
  • Order by the Civil Registrar or Consul General, as the case may be
  • Court's final ORDER/DECISION annulling the marriage

Application for Inclusion of Records in the Book of Voters/Reinstatement of Name in the List of Voters

Requirements

  • Any valid ID previously enumerated that bear applicant's photograph and signature
