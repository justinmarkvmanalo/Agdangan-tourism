About the Office

The Local Youth Development Office (LYDO) serves as the driving force behind youth empowerment and participation in the community. It is a dynamic office dedicated to nurturing the potential, leadership, and civic citizenship of the young people in the municipality.

Through innovative programs, meaningful activities, and strong partnerships with youth organizations such as the Sangguniang Kabataan, schools, and community groups, the LYDO creates opportunities where young people can grow, lead, and make a difference. It champions initiatives in leadership development, volunteerism, skills training, environmental stewardship, and social responsibility.

More than just an office, the LYDO is a platform where the voices, talents, and dreams of the youth are heard and transformed into action. It ensures that every young person becomes an empowered partner in building a progressive, resilient, and inclusive community.

LYDO - empowering the youth today, shaping the leaders of tomorrow.
