About the Office

Property appraisal, tax declaration processing, and records services for the municipality.

The Office of the Municipal Assessor oversees real property appraisal and assessment, maintains property records, and supports tax mapping and valuation functions for Agdangan.

This page follows the same office layout as the Business Permit page, with content adapted to the assessor office references you provided.

Municipal Assessor

Rachel Ann L. Lacuesta

  • Head of the Municipal Assessor's Office.
  • Supervises the appraisal and assessment of real properties for taxation purposes.
  • Ensures the proper implementation of appraisal methods, techniques, and procedures.
  • Prepares and submits assessment reports to higher authorities.
  • Coordinates with national and local agencies regarding property assessment matters.

Assistant Municipal Assessor

  • Assists the Municipal Assessor in daily operations.
  • Oversees the work of units under the office.
  • Takes charge in the absence of the Municipal Assessor.
  • Reviews appraisal and assessment outputs for accuracy and compliance.

Unit and Positional Responsibilities

1. Tax Mapping Operations Unit (Glenn Angelo A. Tapan)

a. Tax Mapper

  • Prepares, updates, and maintains tax maps.
  • Identifies and plots property boundaries.
  • Coordinates with assessors during field verification.

b. Draftsman / Cartographer

  • Draws and updates cadastral and tax maps.
  • Assists in visual representation of real properties.
  • Uses technical drawing tools and software.

c. GIS Operator

  • Utilizes Geographic Information Systems to map and analyze land data.
  • Integrates GIS data with property records.
  • Produces digital mapping outputs for planning and assessment.

2. Appraisal and Assessment Unit (Iven Paul Rolloque)

a. Property Appraiser

  • Conducts physical inspections and appraisals of real properties.
  • Determines fair market value based on prevailing market conditions.
  • Prepares appraisal reports and documentation.

b. Local Assessment Operations Officer

  • Reviews appraisal data and ensures compliance with assessment laws.
  • Updates the tax declaration and assessment roll.
  • Coordinates with barangay officials and property owners.

c. Assessment Clerk

  • Encodes property data into assessment systems.
  • Assists in the issuance of tax declarations and certifications.
  • Maintains property assessment records.

3. Real Property Valuation Unit

a. Property Appraiser

  • Conducts physical inspections and appraisals of real properties.
  • Determines fair market value based on prevailing market conditions.
  • Prepares appraisal reports and documentation.

b. Statistician

  • Collects and analyzes data on property values, market trends, and economic indicators.
  • Assists in zoning and property classification decisions.
  • Prepares statistical reports for planning and decision-making.

c. Assessment Clerk

  • Assists with data entry, file management, and document control related to property valuation.

4. Administrative and Records Management Unit (Nerissa A. Dionco)

a. Administrative Officer

  • Manages office supplies, budget, and personnel matters.
  • Prepares office communications, reports, and schedules.
  • Ensures compliance with administrative policies.

b. Computer Operator / Data Controller

  • Operates computer systems for assessment data processing.
  • Maintains and updates assessment databases.
  • Backs up digital records and ensures data security.

c. Bookbinder / Administrative Aide

  • Binds tax declarations, assessment rolls, and property files.
  • Assists with clerical duties such as filing, sorting, and organizing records.
  • Supports all units in day-to-day administrative tasks.
