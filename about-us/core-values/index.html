About Us

Core values that shape public conduct, service culture, and municipal leadership.

These values define how the Municipality of Agdangan approaches decisions, serves residents, and sustains trust across every office and community-facing function.

View Values How They Apply
Public Service Ethos

Standards that keep governance credible, disciplined, and community-focused.

The page follows your second reference by leaning into the image first, then moving into structured value cards with stronger text hierarchy underneath.

01

Integrity

Act honestly, uphold commitments, and protect public trust in every transaction, recommendation, and decision.

02

Accountability

Own responsibilities clearly, report actions transparently, and remain answerable to the people the municipality serves.

03

Responsiveness

Deliver timely, practical, and respectful service that addresses real community needs without unnecessary delay.

04

Discipline

Maintain order, consistency, and professionalism in daily operations, public conduct, and program implementation.

05

Inclusiveness

Ensure services, participation, and opportunities remain accessible across barangays, sectors, and generations.

06

Stewardship

Manage public resources with care and long-term responsibility for the benefit of the entire municipality.

Daily Application

Core values are only useful when they are visible in actual service delivery.

Agdangan's values should be reflected in frontline conduct, internal coordination, communication with residents, and the way projects are planned and evaluated.

Frontline Service Respectful assistance, clear processes, and dependable response times.
Local Governance Open reporting, lawful procedures, and consistent public accountability.
Community Programs Inclusive implementation that balances order, empathy, and measurable outcomes.
