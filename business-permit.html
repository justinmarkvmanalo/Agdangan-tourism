About the Office

Supporting business registration, renewal, and compliance in Agdangan.

The Business Permits and Licensing Office serves entrepreneurs, store owners, transport operators, and local enterprises by guiding permit processing, renewal requirements, and coordination with regulatory clearances.

The page organizes office information into a clear permit guide with dedicated service, program, update, and contact sections.

Office BPLO Agdangan

New Business Registration

Requirements

  • New Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) of the present year
  • New Barangay Clearance for Business
  • SSS ID and SSS Clearance (w/receipt of the present year)
  • CDA Certificate (For Cooperative only)
  • DTI Registration (For Single Ownership only)
  • SEC Registration (For Corporation only)
  • PAG-IBIG Registration
  • Phil-Health Registration ID (w/receipt of the present year)
  • Installation of CCTV (for businesses with capital of P50,000.00 and above)

Clearances

  • Clearance at Bureau of Fire
  • Clearance at Engineering Office
  • Clearance at MPDC Office
  • Clearance at Assessor Office
  • Clearance at MENRO
  • New Sanitary Permit (Health Center)

Business Permit Renewal

Requirements

  • Accomplished Renewal Application Form
  • Previous Year's Business Permit
  • Community Tax Certificate (CTC)
  • Barangay Clearance
  • Latest Financial Statement

Amendment / Update of Business Information

(Change of business name, address, ownership, nature of business, etc.)

Requirements

  • Letter of Request
  • Supporting documents (DTI/SEC/CDA, Lease Contract, etc.)
  • Current Business Permit

Retirement / Closure of Business

Requirements

  • Letter of Intent to Close
  • Latest Business Permit
  • Payment of Business Tax

Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit

Requirements

  • OR/CR
  • Driver's License
  • Cedula
  • Barangay Clearance
  • Certification of membership issued by the President of TODA
